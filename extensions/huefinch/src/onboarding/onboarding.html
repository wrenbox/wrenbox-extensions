Turn Huefinch on for every website

Huefinch needs access to websites to recolor the pages you visit; it never reads them. It only lays a color filter over each page, in your browser.

No problem. Huefinch still works one tab at a time: click its icon in the toolbar and it recolors that tab until you leave the page. You can turn on automatic mode later from the popup or Settings.
